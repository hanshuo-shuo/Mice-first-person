# Hypothesis

Legally steering from zero to +60 degrees and then holding that pose will
improve clean success over the incumbent on at least two of 128 paired
development episodes without increasing capture episodes.

## Predicted effect

The historical pre-positioned +60-degree reference captured most of the active
camera advantage.  Paying the legal three-step turn cost may retain enough of
that advantage while avoiding the symmetric scan's repeated sweeps.  This
candidate remains distinct from the fixed +60 reference, which is teleported
before the first observation.
