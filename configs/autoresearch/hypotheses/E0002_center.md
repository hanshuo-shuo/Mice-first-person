# Hypothesis

Holding the legally initialized head at the body center by returning zero head
rate will improve clean success over the incumbent on at least two of 128
paired development episodes without increasing capture episodes.

## Predicted effect

If rapid learned gaze motion is mostly control noise, a centered camera should
reduce gaze travel while preserving locomotion quality and improving clean
success.  A tie, a smaller gain, or worse capture mechanically discards this
candidate; lower gaze travel alone is not sufficient.
